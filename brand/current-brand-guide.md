# Micron Fencing Company - Current Brand Guide

## Current identity

Use the latest logo supplied in `assets/logos/`. It combines two fence posts, a twisted barbed-wire M with sharp spikes, and diamond chain-link mesh behind the M.

The earlier PDF in `brand/reference/` records the original strategic and visual proposal. Its simple M logo, logo font identification and early applications predate the latest artwork. This guide and the current SVG assets take precedence for logo implementation. The blue First Fence-inspired exploration is not the active colour direction in this pack.

## Brand message

- Name: Micron Fencing Company
- Proposed tagline: Boundaries. Defined.
- Description: Fencing materials, professional installation and servicing.
- Voice: strong, direct, practical and precise.
- Audience: agricultural landowners, plot owners, housing developers, solar project teams and real estate ventures.

`brand` in filenames means a yellow symbol with white lettering and is intended for dark backgrounds. It is not a separate logo design.

All SVGs contain outlined vector paths with no embedded raster images or external fonts. They are vector traces of the selected visual concept. PNGs are rendered from their matching SVG masters and have true transparency.

Keep the symbol and lettering proportions intact. Leave clear space around the full logo of at least one-quarter of the symbol height. Do not crop the mesh, stretch the artwork, rotate the posts or redraw the wordmark with a substitute font. Keep busy photography away from the logo.

The mesh and wire twists contain fine detail. Check the actual output size for legibility; a separately simplified mark would be needed for a very small favicon or coarse embroidery.

## Typography

The current logo's wordmark is supplied as custom outlined artwork; do not rebuild it by typing MICRON in DejaVu Sans.

For website and document text, DejaVu Sans Regular and Bold are supplied as the proposed supporting font system, together with their licence. They correspond to the supporting type direction in the original brand book.

- Hero: 48-72 px on desktop; 34-44 px on mobile.
- Section headings: 32-40 px on desktop; 26-32 px on mobile.
- Body: 16-18 px; line height about 1.5.
- Short labels: 12-14 px.
- Use uppercase for short headings and labels. Use sentence case for paragraphs and forms.

## Layout and imagery

Use generous white space, straight edges, strong alignment and one main action per section. Use yellow selectively for enquiry actions and emphasis. Keep product specifications easy to scan.

Photography should show actual products, completed fence lines and installation details. Product and site photographs have not been provided and are not included. See `handoff/image-requirements.md` for the shot list.

## Claims and names

Use the supplied product names consistently. Tata, Micron and Sunvik are stated chain-link brand options; this does not establish authorised dealership or partner status. Do not introduce guarantees, years of experience, certifications, client counts, project claims or delivery promises without business confirmation.
