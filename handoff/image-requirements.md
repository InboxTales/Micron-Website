Keep products recognisable, horizons straight and backgrounds simple. Export appropriately sized WebP or AVIF images with fallbacks where required. Write alt text from the actual photograph. Treat the filenames above as a shot list, not existing asset paths.

Do not reuse the reference-site logo or stock-image search screenshots as website assets. If licensed stock is later used, label it as illustrative where it could be mistaken for a Micron project.
