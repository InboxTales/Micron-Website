FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us your site details and our team will help with the next steps.

What fencing products do you supply?

We supply barbed wire, chain link fencing, GI wire, concertina coils, and GI fencing poles.

What options are available for barbed wire and chain link fencing?

Both are available in 120 GSM and 270 GSM options. Contact us to confirm the complete specifications for your requirement.

Which chain link fencing brands are available?

We offer chain link fencing options from Tata, Micron, and Sunvik. Contact us for current availability.

Do you provide fencing installation?

Yes. We provide professional fencing installation for agricultural lands, open plots, housing projects, solar farms, and real estate ventures.

Do you service existing fencing?

Yes. Share your location, photographs, and a description of the work required so we can discuss the servicing scope.

Can I purchase materials without installation?

Yes. Contact us with the products, specifications, and quantities you require.

How is fencing pricing calculated?

Pricing depends on the selected products, specifications, quantities, and installation requirements. Share your site details to request a quotation.

What information should I provide for a quote?

Your site location, property type, approximate boundary length, required service, and available photographs will help us understand the project.
